I run an AI agency that builds AI agents for businesses — agents that **reply, follow up & close, 24/7**.

**Real builds, no gyaan.** I show working systems, not prompt tutorials.

## What I build

- 🤖 **AI agents** that reply to leads instantly — WhatsApp, Instagram, website
- 📞 **AI receptionists** that answer calls and book appointments while you sleep
- 🔁 **Follow-up systems** that chase leads until they book
- ⚙️ **Automations** that run the boring work — CRM updates, reports, outreach

## Who it's for

Startups and small businesses that can't hire a full team yet — an AI team, when you can't hire one.
